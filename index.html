Music Intelligence Project

One of my favorite areas is building systems that understand music taste, metadata, audio features, lyrics, and playlist behavior.

Personal Music Brain

A system idea around discovery, mood analysis, artist data, and smart playlist generation.

What This System Tries To Do

  • Analyze songs using audio, lyrics, metadata, mood, and genre signals
  • Understand listening patterns and personal taste over time
  • Discover better tracks based on artist, vibe, genre, and mood
  • Create smarter playlists instead of random recommendations
  • Combine Spotify data with external datasets and local analysis
music_brain:
  inputs: audio + lyrics + metadata + listening_history
  outputs: mood, genre, vibe, discovery, playlists
  goal: better music recommendations
